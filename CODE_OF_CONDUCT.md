# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to building an open, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone participating in OAEM.

Contributors, maintainers, and participants are expected to engage professionally and in good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and inclusive.
- Focus critique on ideas and artifacts, not people.
- Communicate clearly and constructively.
- Assume good intent while still requiring evidence for technical claims.
- Help keep discussions aligned with project scope and methodology goals.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
- Personal attacks, insults, or hostile behavior.
- Deliberate misinformation about project status, evidence, or governance decisions.
- Publishing sensitive/private information without permission.
- Repeated disruption of productive technical collaboration.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code of conduct.

Responses may include:
- warning and guidance;
- temporary moderation or restricted participation;
- removal of content;
- suspension from participation spaces in severe or repeated cases.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe behavior that violates this code:
- report it to the maintainers through the repository communication channels;
- include links/context where possible.

All reports should be handled with care and discretion.

---

This Code of Conduct is intentionally concise for OAEM v0.1 foundation and may be expanded as the community grows.
