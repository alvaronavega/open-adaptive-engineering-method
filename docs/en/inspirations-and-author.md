# Inspirations and Author

## Inspirations

OAEM was inspired in part by:

- [GitHub Spec Kit](https://github.com/github/Spec-Kit)  
  Demonstrated the value of explicit intent, structured planning, traceable artifacts, and portable quality-oriented workflow framing.

- [Skills for Real Engineers (Matt Pocock)](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills)  
  Demonstrated the value of small adaptable composable Skills, vertical work slicing, practical diagnostics, and test-oriented engineering routines.

OAEM is not a fork or replacement for either project, and it is not endorsed by them.  
It emerged from practical long-running experience applying specification-driven and skill-driven approaches across multi-agent, multi-harness engineering contexts where additional governance and evidence contracts were needed.
