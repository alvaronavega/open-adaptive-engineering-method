# About the Author

## Alvaro Navega

My primary professional perspective comes from Quality Assurance and software quality, rather than from a traditional software-development career path.

I have worked in IT across different agile teams and contexts, collaborating closely with areas such as:
- software development;
- quality engineering;
- test automation;
- agile practices;
- process design;
- cross-functional delivery;
- design-partner style collaboration.

My strongest long-term interests include:
- software-development methodologies;
- test automation;
- quality engineering;
- engineering workflows;
- turning needs into verifiable software.

AI-assisted development and “vibe coding” expanded how deeply I could participate in hands-on software creation, and also exposed recurring practical problems that motivated OAEM.

OAEM is my attempt to organize these lessons into an open methodology that can be criticized, tested, and improved in public.
